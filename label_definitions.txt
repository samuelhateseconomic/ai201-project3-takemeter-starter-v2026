# Milestone 2 taxonomy for public r/ChatGPT submissions.
# Exact definitions also appear in README.md; baseline.py reads these lines.
# Apply one label in this order: analysis, hot_take, question, reaction.
# Full examples, boundary rules, and synthetic stress test: milestone2.md.

analysis: advances a claim, explanation, or recommendation and explicitly supports it with a concrete reported test and result, input/output example, or identifiable source fact relevant to that conclusion.
hot_take: asserts a general judgment, prediction, or recommendation about AI, a model, or its users without the concrete supporting evidence required for analysis.
question: requests a specific explanation, instruction, recommendation, or comparison without advancing a supported conclusion or an unsupported general judgment of its own.
reaction: shares a personal event, feeling, appreciation, or illustrative AI output without a supported conclusion, a general judgment, or a specific request for information or action.
